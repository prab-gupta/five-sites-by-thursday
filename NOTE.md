# Five sites for Thursday

Phone version, with the full score breakdown and sources for every site: https://five-sites-by-thursday.vercel.app

## Bring these five sites on Thursday

Picked from 40 candidates using the rubric agreed with the sponsor. Three come with a condition to check first.

Based on the site tracker export of 21 Sep 2026.

## The five

### 1. Kessby Old Quarry Yard (S-003 · Aldmark) · 97/100

**Check: revenue share**

The most complete case: strong grid capacity close to the substation, a signed letter of intent and support recorded in the council's planning minutes. The open point is money: the owner requests a 12% revenue share. Confirm whether acceptable terms have been agreed.

### 2. Grelle Harbour Plot (S-018 · Nordholm) · 97/100

Matches Kessby on grid capacity and distance, with a signed letter of intent and council committee support. No additional conditions are recorded in the supplied notes. Nordholm reports grid figures in kilowatts; we converted them, and it's worth a quick confirmation with the operator.

### 3. Brekke Depot (S-017/S-031 · Sveld) · 96/100

Plenty of grid capacity, no flood zone recorded in the registry and a signed letter of intent. Neighbours were indifferent: no support recorded, and no opposition recorded either. The team logged it twice under two names; both entries are combined here.

### 4. Varne Farm Edge (S-027 · Tarrow) · 93/100

**Check: noise report**

The best grid connection of the five and a signed letter of intent. The council has no position yet: its support depends on the noise report. Confirm the report's status and outcome.

### 5. Orlund Depot Yard (S-014 · Sveld) · 86/100

**Check: owner agreement**

Good grid capacity, no flood zone recorded in the registry and a ward councillor openly in favour. It ranks fifth because the owner is still at heads of terms with no letter of intent yet, and the cable run is a little longer.

## Why not these

### Esk Mill Land · 100/100

S-009 · would score this if it weren't excluded

**Left out: possible nature reserve**

On paper it's the best site. But a field note says part of the plot is inside a protected nature reserve. The reserve boundary needs verification before the site can be reconsidered. We can't build in a reserve, so it stays excluded until then.

> Municipal ecologist says the northern third of the plot falls inside the Esker Meadows reserve designated in 2024.

### Rook Street Plot · 82/100

S-013

**Left out: plot too small**

The land registry says 2,400 m², but that record is from 2017. A recent field note says half was sold and only about 700 m² remains. A 2 MW battery needs about 1,200 m².

> roughly 700 m² remains

### Pellick Lane Plot · 45/100

S-024 · 100 if everything checks out

**Left out: not enough evidence**

It has one of the strongest grid figures (5 MW, 0.6 km away). But there is no land registry record and no field note, so we don't know the owner, the plot size, the flood risk or local opinion. Worth a visit, not a pitch.

## How sure we are

"6/6 known" means every part of the rubric has a value from a source. It does not mean the site is confirmed feasible. None of these sites has had a feasibility study; that is exactly what the sponsor would pay for.

- Grid figures are the official ones, published quarterly (these are from June 2026).
- Letters of intent are non-binding.
- The notes on council and neighbours are the team's own reports.
- Where a figure was missing we scored it zero, not an average. "If everything checks out" assumes missing parts get full marks; known values stay as they are.

## Takeaways

1. Bring these five: Kessby Old Quarry Yard, Grelle Harbour Plot, Brekke Depot, Varne Farm Edge, Orlund Depot Yard. These are recommendations for feasibility studies, not confirmed buildable sites.
2. Confirm Kessby's revenue-share terms and the status of Varne's noise report.
3. Confirm the next step toward a signed owner agreement at Orlund.
4. Confirm the reserve boundary at Esk Mill (S-009). If it's outside, it's the strongest site.
5. Look up Pellick Lane (S-024) and Gorse Hill (S-036) in the land registry and find their owners.
